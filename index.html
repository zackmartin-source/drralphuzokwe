About

Built it, lost some, built again.

Nigeria raised me, America tested me, and thirty plus ventures taught me the one thing that never appears in a business plan: comfort kills faster than competition.

You only fail when you give up.

I studied at night, worked through the day, and put whatever was left into the first business, then the next one. Some worked in a month. Some took years. A few closed, and those taught me the most, because a business that dies tells you exactly where you stopped paying attention.
